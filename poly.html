Click = load · Shift+click = save · Cmd/Ctrl+click = add to a chain · Alt+click = erase. Move: hold Loop + step = load (several steps = chain), hold Duplicate + step = save.

live record · play the pads, they stick

Armed + playing: pads (or keys 1–8 / q–i / a–k / z–,) play their row's lane and write the nearest step. Hard hits = accents. Move: Record arms, Shift+Record = tap tempo.

evolve · the pattern drifts on its own

Every N bars some lanes get nudged (rotation, fill, length, speed). Untick a lane to keep it steady. Move: Shift+Capture toggles.

dice · free or repeatable randomness

Locked: probability, humanize, evolve and randomize follow the seed, so the groove repeats exactly.

what each parameter does

Gauges: drag up/down (Shift = fine), scroll, or arrow keys; double‑click resets. At speed ×1 a step is a beat, so 4 steps = one bar; ×2 = eighths, ×4 = sixteenths, ½ = half notes. Lengths 3 · 4 · 5 · 7 at the same speed = polymeter; ratios ⅔ · 3/2 · 3 = true polyrhythm. Euclid spreads k hits evenly; rotation shifts them.
