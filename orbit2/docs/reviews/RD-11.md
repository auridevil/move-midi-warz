## A10 screen-off drift by the player agents (2026-11-23): FAIL, 0 / 5 per player

**How it ran** (`orbit2/tools/moveview.mjs a10`, new; pure parts `session-core.mjs a10Trials / scoreA10`, unit-tested).
Page code `add4e5a`, `orbit2/index.html?drift`, techno groove (4 lanes), the Move simulated, muted Chrome on
5400 / 9400. Each try starts from another hand's drift put there through the Move (every 2–16, amount 10–80 %, a
lane set that always needs a tap, evolve on or off); then the hand sets Noor's target with only the lights to go by:
evolve on by the − LED, play by the play LED, hold −, touch knob 1, count the meter, turn, count again until 8; the
same on knob 2 (3 clicks an LED) until 8 of 16; lanes 1, 2, 4 in and 3 out by the step buttons. Playing on, it waits
until the ear is a set number of bars before the next mutation (0.35 bar inside one countdown LED, a different one
each try) and draws three pictures: − held (the table), − held + a finger on knob 1, − held + a finger on knob 2. The
truth is the model as set and the mutation the clock really made next (`m.evolve` / `m.assign` wrapped: its bar line
against the heard tick of picture 1). **Every try reached the target** (every 8, amount 48 / 50 / 52 %, meters 8 / 8,
lanes 1 2 4), and the real mutation came within 0.01 bar of where the countdown said (asked 2.65 → 2.644 … 6.65 →
6.646): the countdown is the clock's. Judges: one fresh sub-agent per try (sonnet, the noor or ines persona), shown
the brief (`A10_BRIEF`: the three Move-table rows of `index.html`, which picture is which hand) and its three

**Why it fails: a steady lane is not dark.** `app.js paintLeds` lights a steady lane's step LED in its dim lane colour
(`LANE_DIM`): lane 3 steady draws olive `#90821f` (relative luminance 0.22; an off LED is 0.011, lane 3 evolving
0.72). Nils's LED table (`proposals/nils/answers.md` §Q1: "lanes that evolve in their lane colour, **steady lanes
dark**") and our own Move table ("lane colour; **dark = steady**") both say dark, so all ten judges read four lit LEDs
as four lanes evolving; their notes say so ("LEDs 1–4 are lit and 5–8 are dark … so lanes 1–4 evolve"; "I went by lit
versus dark"). Everything the meter and the countdown carry reads right in 10 of 10: the meters' 8 LEDs as every = 8
bars and 8 / 16 = 50 %, the lit countdown LEDs counted exactly, and the bars named = the LEDs lit (the truth is 0.35
bar under that: one LED a bar at every = 8). **A10 is open: a defect of this phase's LED table against its spec**
(in scope, round 1). `?drift` stays until it passes.

Judges' other notes (for the panel, not scored): the two meters look alike (8 and 8), only the hand knows which knob it
touched; the step LEDs' lane colours do not match the pads' (the pads show a lane's empty steps dim, its hits bright,
its accents white), so several judges went by position rather than colour.

Not counted: a 2-try dry run (seed 9) that checked the tool (pictures looked at by the implementer, never shown to a
judge), and two runs cut short when `src/dev-reload.js` reloaded the page because a repo file was edited during the run
(the tool now fails a try whose page reloaded instead of counting it).
