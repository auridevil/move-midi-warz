## What shipped (for the panel)

- `?drift` (URL flag, like `?setlog`): hold − = the drift page. Knobs 1–8 = every · amount · gravity · home after ·
  max cycle · coupling lift · humanize · dice repeat (the sheet's own gauges, moved in by bindRow). Step LEDs 1–6 =
  the lanes that evolve (tap = toggle, a flagged chord); 9–16 drain toward the next mutation, one per ⅛ of "every";
  a touched (or just-turned) drift encoder turns all 16 into its meter (counts 1 LED each, percentages in 16ths).
  The on-screen cells mirror the LED table from the same pure function (`pages.js driftLeds`). Tap − still toggles
  evolve, now on the release and only when nothing was turned or tapped while − was down (approved 2026-11-14).
- Flag off: the mapping is byte-identical (− toggles evolve on the press; static + scripted identity tests).
- Move table in `index.html`: 2 new rows marked "(with ?drift)", the − row names the release timing.
