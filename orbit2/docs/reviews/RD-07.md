# RD-07 · Pills: review panel

Reviewers: verifier (Vera), architect (Arjun), sound (Sofia), maya, greta, kai. Process: [[REVIEW-PANEL]] (redesign
phases: the core three + the three named in the decision's row). Spec: [[DECISION]] (`orbit2/docs/redesign/DECISION.md`
§1 principle 4 "state is always visible", §2 "The screen at 1512×860" (the right column: the Move pill and "?", then the
pills, then the sheet under the last one), "Pills come on top of that: up to 4 are shown, each a button with a ×, and
any more fold into '+N more ▾'", "How it degrades" (1280×720: at most 4 pills, the sheet never shorter than 340 px;
phone: pills become one line), the disagreements rows "F mode and pills" (RD-9's) and "Quest 'show me'", §3 row RD-7).
Acceptance: **each pill announced once, its text never updated per bar (A4 part)**; **1280×720 with 6 pills keeps the
sheet ≥ 340 px**; **A7 still passes**. Must not regress: quest progression (I6), welcome back (F3), jam takes (download
/ discard), pending-mute timing, MIDI clock follow.

What the phase built (`6e458fc` pill region, then the tests-and-browser-check commit):
- `#pills` (`role="status"`) at the top of the right column, one fixed node per pill in the decision's order: rec armed
  (· touch / latch) · chain, or a slot / mutes waiting for the bar · solo · evolve · dice · clock follow · welcome back ·
  quest · jam. Each pill is a joined bar: its first button shows the mode's control in the sheet (or does the pill's job),
  × turns the mode off (not on a queued slot / mute, which only waits; the jam take has download / discard instead).
- Pure model `src/poly/pills.js` (`pillText`, `pillsOn`, `pillLayout`: ≤ 4 drawn, the rest fold into "+N more ▾";
  `pillOff`). `app.js renderPills` never moves a node (a fold is a class, the dropped-down position a CSS variable) and
  writes a word or attribute only when it changed; the words carry no clock counter (the take's beats stay on the jam
  word and the canvas). The welcome bar, the quest card and the jam panel are pills now, their ids kept.
- The quest pill opens a drop-down (`aria-live="off"`) with its hint, **show me** and skip. Show me: `scene.js ghost`
  draws the gesture once as a ghost hand (`ghostAt`: a pull out from the selected ring for 3-against-4, a turn along the
  snare's ring for the backbeat; read-only, any key or press hides it, reduced motion: no travel); the evolve, slot and
  export quests show their control in the sheet instead.
- The Move pill stays in the top row beside "?" ("Move · live / edit" once connected) and does not count toward the 4.
- "+N more" and the quest hint drop down over the sheet, so the sheet never shrinks for them; both are popups in the
  one Esc order.
