- **Knob rows by page binding** (`gauge.js bindRow`): the row shows the sheet's own gauges, moved into its 8 slots;
  each leaves a marker where it lived and goes back there when the row lets it go (the same gauge objects, so A3's
  onStart / onEnd, the "A" marks and what a held hand shows come with them). rhythm = length · hits · shift · speed ·
  swing · chance · tune · tempo (the Move's knobs, `RHYTHM_KEYS`, also used by the encoder handler); sound = Shift +

  the step (the Move's hold-step knobs 1–7; knob 8, the condition, stays in the sheet until RD-6). Knob values 22 px /
  500 (15 px at 1280×720, where a knob is 74 px wide). A knob the pointer holds never leaves the row mid-drag.
- **The page model** (`pages.js resolvePage`, pure; `test/console.test.js`): Nils's state table. A held (Shift, a step
  looked at: `LOOK_MS` or a knob turned) or touched (an encoder, + `TOUCH_HOLD_MS` 400 ms after) Move control owns the
  row; let go, the row returns to the screen's latch (a tab, ⇧ held on the keyboard, or a cell's long-press = step),
  never to rhythm; a step not yet looked at owns nothing (a tap never flashes a page); the chip names what the Move's
  knobs edit, grey when that is not what is drawn, white when the Move owns the row; Loop held = the cells' page is
  "slots" (drawn by RD-6). `app.js pageNow / renderPage` feed it and draw it: the held cell is outlined at once, a Move
  look that edited nothing gives the lock editor back as it was, a cell's long-press latches the step page (Esc closes

- **The peek** (left column, under the lane list): 64 px weight 300 number, "knob 6 · chance · conga", the plain

- **The lane list** (`#legend`, left column): a row per lane (dot, voice, length · ×speed: a click selects it) and its
