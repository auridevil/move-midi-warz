What the phase changed:
- **The step page is the one lock editor** (console, `app.js openStep / editLocks / renderLocks`). It shows when a Move
  step is held `LOOK_MS` (450 ms) or a knob is touched / turned on it (the cell is outlined in the press's own task), or
  when a cell or a planet is long-pressed, right-clicked or Alt+clicked (the mouse latch; the sheet stays closed). Its
  8 knobs are the step's lock gauges drawn as **rings** (`gauge.js` shape `ring`: the track a whole circle in the
  lane's ink, solid where the step is locked, dashed where it follows the lane; 6 px like an arc's track): tune ·
  probability · velocity · decay · tone · snap · color, and **knob 8 = the condition** (CONDS order, as the Move's hold
  step + knob 8), or **the repeats while the Move holds the step with Shift** (T2: only slot 8 repaints;
  `resolvePage().row8`). The header shows **clear · more… · done** where "steps 1–16 of 16" was: clear = the step's
  locks, condition and repeats; more… = Sheet → lane, the old lock panel, now the rest (nudge, stroke, slice, start /
  end, direction, the condition's loop, the repeats' spacing, fade, pitch ramp); done (or Esc, or the same Alt+click)
  closes it.
- **The cells follow the page** (`renderSteps` → `renderDots`): on a step page the 16 cells are the step's **loop dots**
  (`pages.js stepLoops`, the same function the Move's step LEDs use while a step is looked at: lit = plays on that loop,
  dashed = depends on fill or the step before, a ring = the loop playing now, moving every loop); with **Loop held** on
  the Move they are the **16 slots** (lit = saved, ring = playing, dashed = queued, outlined = picked for a chain). The
  cell keeps its box; only the dot inside changes. What a click does follows what is drawn (`pages.js cellAction`): a
  step cycles; a loop dot opens that cell's step page (switches the step, never an edit); a slot does nothing. With a
  step page latched, a click on a planet switches the page to that step (`planetAction`).
- **The radial menu is gone** (`openRingMenu`, `#ring-menu`, its CSS, popup and find hooks): a planet's long-press /
  right-click opens its step page instead.
- **The ring answers the hand** (`scene.js setHand`, pure `HAND_LOOK / shortSteps / pushSteps / newTicks`): while a knob
  is touched (Move) or dragged (console or sheet), and `HAND_MS` (= `TOUCH_HOLD_MS`, 400 ms) after, the selected ring
  shows what the change did, read-only: shift slides the planets (eased, `SLIDE_MS` 160) with a short trail; hits draw a
  ring leaving every planet once; swing draws the off-beats where `Lane.swingAt` says they sound; chance draws an arc
  round each planet as long as its chance; push moves the ring by `offsetMs / stepMs` steps with an arc at 12 o'clock;
  a longer loop's new ticks pulse once; **a held step gets a halo** (a 17 px ring: Move-held steps, and the step a
  step-page knob edits while a hand is on it). Reduced motion: no glide, no growing rings (the trail and fades only).
  `locate` (the hit test) is untouched: nothing new is clickable. An idle page still draws nothing. **The touched knob
  gets a lane-colour plate** (`#knobs > i.plate`, while the peek shows for a touch, drag or turn); no echo bubble.
- **Tools and tests:** `test/steppage.test.js` (Nils's table T0–T4 every column, every edge case, the loop dots, the
  click rules, static guards), `test/hand.test.js` (each knob's answer, the slide / push / new ticks maths, read-only
  and reduced-motion guards); new `tools/step-check.mjs` (MOVE, TAP, MOUSE, LOOP, HAND incl. the halo, scripted A2 for
  the step page, FRAME); `face-audit.mjs` gains a **step page** state (A6 / A7 + A1 measured with the page open, in the
  everything view); `console-check.mjs` reads the held cell by index (loop dots have other names); `find-check.mjs`
  phone CLICK fixed (see below) and says what changed when a click edits.

Found and fixed during this check:
- **The held step's halo was never drawn** (`ce4f168`): an edit had put a `//` comment in the middle of the line that
  draws it (RD-02's lesson). The new HAND halo count fails on `ce4f168` and passes now.

  with 8 / 2.5 dashes the dashed condition knob with no value drawn reads 3.01:1 (min).
- **"Flashes" from rewriting the same value**: `renderPage` rewrote `hidden` / `data-cells` every call; it writes only
  on a change now (cheaper too).
- **find-check phone CLICK** ("a click on empty canvas edited something", also on `ec0855c`): opening the share menu
  scrolled the phone page, so the found spot sat on a ring and the second click selected a lane. The tool scrolls back,
  waits for focus mode's resize before finding a spot, and drags from a spot whose 30 px stay off the rings; it now
  says what changed.
