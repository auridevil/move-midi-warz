### Fixed during the browser check

- **A hand edit made in the same task as a named action was swallowed** (a Move step + knob turn right before
  `clearLane()` never reached the log; an earlier mute's absorb flag also swallowed later edits). Now an action that
  replaces the pattern first diffs the waiting hand edits (`slog.begin()`), and only the 12 actions that change the
  pattern absorb their own diff, by re-snapshotting the pattern at once (`act(…, { absorb: true })`, never from the
  clock); tempo, filter, evolve, record, slot save and exports absorb nothing. Slot recall and chain now log after their
  load. +1 test.
