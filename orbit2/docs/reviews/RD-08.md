# RD-08 · Light on the canvas: review panel

Reviewers: verifier (Vera), architect (Arjun), sound (Sofia), noor, yuki, ines. Process: [[REVIEW-PANEL]] (redesign
phases: the core three + the three named in the decision's row). Spec: [[DECISION]] (`orbit2/docs/redesign/DECISION.md`
§1 principles 3 "the sun is a display, not a button", 4, 6 and 7; §2 "Read-only light on the canvas" (the sun numeral,
"press play" and the fill flare, the cycle caption in straight type, the generative arc, the slot moons, the one arc
label), the disagreements rows "Numeral weight" (300 only at 40 px and up), "The countdown" (it moves continuously,
steps once per beat with reduced motion; the evolve pill reads "evolve" and does not repeat it), "Arc labels" (one label,
selected lane only, 13 px caps weight 500, 78 % white, the bottom of the ring within ±35°; numbers never on a curve;
Yuki's drop order) and "First visit"; §3 row RD-8). Yuki's ladder and floors: `proposals/yuki/answers.md` §1.
Acceptance: **A9** (frame p95 ≤ RD-1 + 1 ms, the audio hash identical); **the 6-lane / 32-step drop order matches
Yuki's list**; **label floors 13 px word / 15 px number**. Must not regress: frame time and the stress meter while
playing; reduced motion; nothing on the canvas becomes clickable.

What the phase built (`b6e15fd` canvas light, then the tests-and-browser-check commit), all in `scene.js` unless said:
- **The sun**: the beats until everything lines up (40 px weight 300, 28 px weight 400 inside a phone's small inner
  ring, "beats" under it lit on the last beat) over a soft dark plate so it reads on a white-hot core; stopped, the
  cycle's length in beats. **"press play"** there on a visit that has never played (`HINT_MS` 7 s, a cut with reduced
  motion; the play ring's pulse slowed to 2.6 s). **The fill flare**: a brighter core and a four-point star while fill
  is held from anywhere (button, key 0, the Move's Layout, a latched bar). The old core pill ("aligns in N bars") is gone.
- **The generative arc** over the top: words only (EVOLVE · DICE · COUPLING NEXT | MATRIX · GRAVITY: numbers are never set
  on a curve, so the seed and the countdown live in the straight caption) and a crawl that moves continuously toward the
  next mutation (`genProgress` from the last bar line `app.js barAt`, a slightly negative fraction right after the
  scheduled line so it never jumps; once per beat with reduced motion).
- **The slot moons** under the rings (slot 1 at the left, ±42° round 6 o'clock): saved = a moon, empty = a ring, the
  playing one bright, the queued one in a dashed ring; numbers by the ladder (all at 13 px / the playing and queued at
  15 px / none).
- **One arc label** on the selected lane (`laneLabel`: "CONGA 32 × 4", the name in the lane's colour, the rest 78 % white,
  13 px caps weight 500, tracked .14 em) in the middle of the gap outside its ring (inside for the outer lane; at most
  37 px out), its extras (hits, chance, push) while they fit the ±35° band and the ring is not a full strip (over 16
  steps: the name-plate alone, as both 32-step lanes in Yuki's mock).
- **The straight caption** (13 px, bottom right of the stage cell): the cycle ("cycle · bar 7 of 16", the length when
  stopped, beats when the cycle is not whole bars), "evolve in N bars", "dice 4127", the playing slot when the moons
  carry no number, the take's beat (the jam arc moved to just outside the outer ring, whole at 1280×720; its count was
  drawn off-screen before). Lines go from the end while the block would touch the rings (a phone).
- **Yuki's ladder** (`lightLevel` / `lightPlan`, pure): when space runs out things go in order (unselected labels,
  extras, slot numbers, arc detail, words off the curve), the lowest rung gone taking every rung above it. Space is
  measured: the px the band carries vs the words, the moons' spacing vs a number, and `LIGHT_ROOM` (40 px) outside the
  outer ring for anything with words (1512×860 has 43; 1280×720 has 12 by the decision's 568 px rings, so the crawl and
  the moons hug the ring there and the words stay in the caption).
- Every word is a **sprite** (`arcSprite` glyph by glyph on its tangent, `textSprite`; at most 32, made again once the
  fonts load): no `fillText` per frame. `__poly.scene.light` reports what the last frame measured and drew (level, room,
  plan, label, arc words, caption lines, the hint, the sun's text, the crawl).
- `pills.js`: the evolve pill reads "evolve". `cdp.mjs open({ reducedMotion })`. `setStage` takes the stage cell too.
- Read-only: `locate` is untouched, the scene listens to no pointer (unit-tested), a click on the sun, a moon, the arc's
  words or the caption changes nothing (browser-tested).
