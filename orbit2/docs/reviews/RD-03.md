What the phase changed:
- **Grid** (`orbit2/styles.css`, wide windows ≥ 1101 px): the body is a grid, `300 px · stage · 428 px` over a
  `144 px` console row at 1512 wide; up to 1400 px wide (DECISION's 1280×720 rule) `256 · stage · 380` over `128`. Two
  fixed rows, every column one clipping cell, so nothing inside a column can resize the stage. The top strip sits in
  the console row until RD-5's console replaces it. Narrow windows (≤ 1100 px) keep their stacked page.
- **Rings** (`scene.js ringBox(cell, winW)`, replacing `stageBox` + `app.js layoutHud()`): from the empty `#stage` grid
  cell and the window width only. D = min(w − 76, h − 24, max(630, 0.88 h)); centred on the window when that ring fits
  12 px clear of both columns, else in the cell. Measured: (756, 358) ⌀ 630 at 1512×860, (578.5, 296.5) ⌀ 568 at
  1280×720, (691.5, 418.5) ⌀ 708 at 1512×982 (taller and wider: it no longer fits centred on the window, so it centres
  in the cell). Focus mode no longer moves them.
- **The sheet** (`index.html`, `app.js setSheet / reveal`): the 13 drawers and the old right column moved, markup
  unchanged and ids kept, into `#sheet` with six tabs (Greta's feature table): lane = lane chips + lane drawer; pattern
  = init … clear lane, grooves, Patterns, Library; groove = Humanize, Evolve, Dice; perform = Record; export = Export;
  setup = the simple / everything switch, Settings, MIDI, the Move table, Nerd stats, the stress meter. Welcome, quest
  and the jam panel stay above it (RD-7's pills go there). Wide: pinned open, the right column's full width, never under
  340 px tall; × closes it to its tab row. Narrow: a bottom sheet, docked closed, full height once a tab is tapped (the
  phone's play floats over it). Roving tabindex, ← → Home End; a one-drawer tab opens its drawer; the quest light also
  marks its tab; `midi-warz.orbit2.sheet` remembers the tab (and open / closed on wide windows). `reveal(el)` opens the
  tab and drawers holding a control: the lock editor (a planet's long-press), the library (load / ⌘O), session.mjs.
- **Tests and tools**: `test/sheet.test.js` (tabs, the 13 drawers in their tabs, old columns gone), `poly.test.js` ring
  box at four window sizes, `face-audit.test.js` (the baseline's 62 ids, the IDS verdict), dark-room recorder check
  order-free. `face-audit.mjs`: IDS gate (`--ids`, default the RD-1 baseline), A6 / A7 states per tab, sheet closed and
  pills, every pane shown for the everything / type / hue pass, a docked bottom bar clips what it covers.
  `session.mjs center()` uses `__poly.reveal`.
