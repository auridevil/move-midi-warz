The replays came out **accident for accident as the baseline** (the redesign so far changed no outcome of these
scripts: they are Move-first, and the Move mapping is unchanged by decision). Take 1 found four things:

1. **The transport's clock breaks under knob drags (page errors, and a page that hangs).** kai-3 and kai-6 threw
   `TypeError: Cannot read properties of undefined (reading 'time')` in Tone's `TickSource.getTicksAtTime ←
   forEachTickBetween ← Clock._loop` (2 and 6 times); noor-5 stopped answering CDP for 120 s twice (each a lost set).
   Replaying kai-6's own script with a tripwire per action (`/tmp/orbit2-impl/err-repro.mjs`) put both errors right
   after a mouse drag on knob 4, **speed ×** (a stray-knob drag). Cause: the ratio setter calls `retime()`, which ramps
   the transport's bpm (`bpm.rampTo(tempo, 0.1)`) even when the tempo did not change, so a drag (a step every 25 ms)
   overlaps 100 ms ramps; the tempo knob's own drag does the same with real changes. In the vendored Tone,
   `rampTo → setRampPoint → Param.cancelAndHoldAtTime` adds a `setValueAtTime` event with no `ticks` to the bpm's
   `TickParam` (its own `setValueAtTime` / `linearRamp…` compute them), and the clock's `getTimeOfTick` searches those
   events by ticks; a ramp between two nearly equal values also makes its quadratic cancel to 0 and take the other
   root. The clock's next tick then lands before 0 (the TypeError: that block's ticks are dropped) or does not move
   (`for (; h < e;)` never ends: the page hangs). Measured in a page (`/tmp/orbit2-impl/ramp-repro.mjs`, 40 bursts of
   8 calls 25 ms apart while playing): `rampTo` hung the page (no answer in 60 s); a cancel + linear ramp through the
   current value still threw 11 times per run; a **step** (`bpm.value = v`, what the MIDI-clock paths already do)
   threw 0 times in 3 runs, ticks steady and never backwards. The ratio setter's `retime()` dates from the first commit
   (2026-10-01): the shipped Orbits has it too.
2. **6 of the 7 destructive accidents are one bounce:** the kick muted again **96–174 ms after a press that unmuted

### What changed after take 1

- **The transport's tempo is a step.** `retime()` sets `bpm.value` (cancel + `setValueAtTime` at `now()`, what the MIDI
  clock paths already did), never `bpm.rampTo`; the ratio setter no longer calls `retime()` (a ratio changes no tempo
  and no delay time). The delays still glide (plain params). Guard: `console-check.mjs` **CLOCK** — 30 mouse drags (as
  `session.mjs` drags: 8 moves 25 ms apart) on knob 4 (speed ×) and knob 8 (tempo) and 30 Move turns of the same knobs
  while playing: the page must answer, throw nothing, its ticks never go back. HEAD: 60 / 60, 0 errors, 0 steps back;
  the same tool on `505c7e0` (`--root`): **the page stopped answering after 9 drags**.
- **A mute press that bounces** (`perform.js MuteBounce`, `BOUNCE_MS` 200, unit-tested): a press that would mute a lane
  less than 0.2 s after a press that unmuted it (or asked the bar line to) is dropped; every mute path goes through
  `toggleMute` (Move Mute + pad / track, a Mute tap, the M lamps, the console's mute). Only that direction, so a quick
  mute → unmute (a stutter) still does both and a dropped press always leaves the lane sounding (principle 3). The Move
  table's Mute row says so. Guard: `console-check.mjs` **BOUNCE** (the Move double bring-back, a double click on the
  kick's M, "on next bar" → the kick keeps playing; a stutter → 2 presses, ends unmuted; a press 0.3 s after an unmute
  mutes): HEAD all as expected; `505c7e0` fails exactly the three bounce cases.
- **Hold Loop names its page** (`pages.js resolvePage`): the header line says "slots · held on Move" and hides "steps
  N–M of L" while the cells are the slots (Nils §Q3: a page repaints the cells and the header line; a step page keeps
  its own word and tools). Unit test (`steppage.test.js`, Nils's edge cases) and `step-check.mjs` LOOP.
- `session.mjs` notes on each action the page errors that came with it (take 1 needed a replay to find them).

Checks on the fixed code: `npm run check` clean · `npm test` **527 + 9** · smoke **10 / 10** · console-check all pass
