    At speed ×1 a step is a beat, so 4 steps = one bar; ×2 = eighths, ×4 = sixteenths, ½ = half notes. Lengths 3 · 4 · 5 · 7 at the same speed = polymeter; ratios ⅔ · 3/2 · 3 = true polyrhythm; 5/4 · 7/4 = fives and sevens over four beats, 4/3 = hemiola, ×6 = sixteenth triplets. Euclid spreads k hits evenly; rotation shifts them.

    On the orbits: each planet is a hit you set, as solid as its chance of playing (a faint one seldom plays). A ring that flashes round a planet: it came round and stayed silent (solid ring: its chance said no · dotted: the locked dice said no · dashed: its condition). Arcs round a planet: a 1:3-style condition, one arc per loop from the top; the solid arc is the loop it plays on, the white one the loop you are in now. The thin arc outside the rings: how far you are through the whole cycle, until everything lines up again.

    Strokes (step locks) play neighbouring notes: the lane's note for the first stroke, then the next notes up that no other lane or stroke uses on the same channel, so a slap never plays another lane's drum. With the default notes a conga on lane 1 plays open 36, mute 37, slap 40 (38 and 39 are lanes 2 and 4). Live out and the MIDI export alike.
